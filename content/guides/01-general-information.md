Targeted marketing means showing the right message to the right people, then using data to make it work better. In Japan it is the fastest-growing part of advertising, and companies hire beginners into it every year. This series of eight guides takes you from no experience to your first marketing job in about six months.

## Why now

In 2025, internet advertising passed half of Japan's total ad spend for the first time. The fastest-growing parts, social media and short video, are exactly where targeted marketing skills are used.

| Segment (Dentsu, 2025) | Spend | Year on year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total ad spend | ¥8.06 trillion | +5.1% |
| Internet advertising | ¥4.05 trillion (50.2% of total) | +10.8% |
| Social media ads | ¥1.31 trillion | +18.7% |
| Video ads | ¥1.03 trillion | +21.8% |
| TV, newspaper, magazine and radio combined | ¥2.30 trillion | −1.6% |

Analysts forecast internet ad spend will grow another 8.3% in 2026, led by video and social ([Dentsu](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202603045005), [CARTA/Dentsu/Septeni](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202603034899)).

**What this means for you:** the jobs are in performance (運用型) ads on social media and video. Skills on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LINE are worth more to employers than general marketing theory.

## Platforms to know in Japan

Japan's platform mix is different from other countries. Beyond Google and Instagram, know these:

- **LINE** — official accounts and LINEヤフー広告 reach people inside the messaging app most Japanese people use every day.
- **Yahoo! JAPAN** — search and display ads still run alongside Google, and job listings name Yahoo!広告 as a required skill.
- **X (Twitter)** — far more central in Japan than in most countries.
- **TVer and connected TV** — part of the fast-growing video category, and already appearing in senior job requirements.

## Three rules every marketer in Japan must know

| Rule | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| ステマ規制 (stealth marketing ban, since 1 Oct 2023) | Under 景品表示法, ads disguised as genuine opinion are illegal. Sponsored posts need clear labels such as #PR or 【広告】. The advertiser is liable. The first enforcement order (June 2024) involved Google Maps reviews traded for discounts ([ISSOH](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/4794/)). |
| 外部送信規律 (cookie rule, since June 2023) | The amended Telecommunications Business Act requires many sites to disclose what user data, such as cookies and ad IDs, they send to third parties ([創業手帳](https://sogyotecho.jp/cookie-impact/)). |
| 個人関連情報 (privacy law, since April 2022) | The amended APPI restricts passing cookie-type data to a company that will link it to a named person ([Web担](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/e/2023/05/19/44501)). |

If you can explain the stealth-marketing rule in an interview, you will sound more experienced than most applicants.

## Words you will see often

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 運用型広告 | Performance ads you adjust daily, such as search and social ads |
| リスティング広告 | Search ads shown next to Google or Yahoo! results |
| インプレッション (imp) | How many times an ad was shown |
| CTR (クリック率) | Clicks ÷ impressions |
| CVR (コンバージョン率) | Purchases or sign-ups ÷ clicks |
| CPA (顧客獲得単価) | Ad cost ÷ number of purchases or sign-ups |
| ROAS (広告費用対効果) | Sales from ads ÷ ad cost |
| KPI | The main number a team agrees to track |
| LP (ランディングページ) | The page people land on after clicking an ad |
| ペルソナ | A detailed picture of the target customer |

## Keep learning every day

Read one article a day on [Web担当者Forum](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/) or [MarkeZine](https://markezine.jp/), the two main Japanese marketing news sites. Once a week, write one sentence about what changed in the industry. These notes make good talking points in interviews.
