---
title: Paid Schools and Funding
summary: When a paid school is worth it, which route fits your situation, and government programs that refund up to 80%.
---
Pay for a school only if you need what free courses can't give you: real client work for your portfolio and help finding a job. Two government programs can return 70–80% of the fee, but only for approved courses, and only if you apply before the course starts.

## Which route fits you

| Your situation | Best route |
| --- | --- |
| Not working, and not receiving unemployment benefits | Hello Training 求職者支援訓練: free (Guide 3) |
| Not working, and receiving unemployment benefits | Ask Hello Work about 公共職業訓練: free |
| Working now, and want to change jobs | METI reskilling program: up to 70% back |
| You have paid into employment insurance for long enough | 教育訓練給付金: up to 80% back |
| You learn well alone and want to spend nothing | Free courses only (Guide 3) |

Not sure which applies to you? Ask at your local Hello Work. They can check your employment insurance record.

## Government programs that pay you back

| Program | Who it's for | Most you get back | Key condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [リスキリングを通じたキャリアアップ支援事業](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/187/4187571/) (METI) | People who are working now (在職者) and want to change jobs | Up to 70% | Must use an approved provider that combines career counseling, training and job-change support |
| [専門実践教育訓練給付金](https://www.agaroot.jp/senmon_kyuufu/) (MHLW) | Workers with enough employment insurance history | 50% when you finish, +20% if you get hired, +10% if your pay rises 5% or more. Up to 80%, capped at ¥640,000 a year | Register at Hello Work before the course starts |
| [特定一般 / 一般教育訓練給付金](https://money-lifehack.com/article/2410) (MHLW) | Same, for shorter courses | 50% (cap ¥250,000) / 20% (cap ¥100,000) | Approved courses only |

## Schools to look at

| School | What stands out | Government refund |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wannabe Academy](https://exidea.co.jp/blog/study/web-marketing/web-marketing-school-recommend/) (ワナビーアカデミー) | 4-month course plus 2 months of real practical work; job-change support | Eligible; cited at about ¥39,000 after refunds for working adults |
| [WEBMARKS](https://exidea.co.jp/blog/study/web-marketing/webmarks/) (ウェブマークス) | Ads plus SEO and content in one 6-month program | Ask the school |
| [MERC Education](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/r/prtimes/items/000000006.000129949) | 3-month marketing strategy and web marketing courses | Both the METI program and 専門実践 (up to 80%) |
| デジプロ, マケキャン (DMM) | Focused on ad operations, with job support | Ask the school |

Most Japanese “best school” rankings online are paid affiliate pages. Treat them as lists of names, not as reviews. A 2026 survey by ALC also found schools whose websites claimed refund eligibility for courses that weren't approved ([ALC](https://www.alc.co.jp/programming/?p=15975)).

## Before you enroll

- [ ] Find the exact course on MHLW's 教育訓練講座検索システム to confirm it is approved
- [ ] Register at Hello Work before the course start date, if you are using a refund program
- [ ] Ask whether the course includes real client work you can put in your portfolio
- [ ] Ask what job support includes and how many graduates found marketing jobs
- [ ] Get the total cost and the refund conditions in writing
- [ ] Wait a day and talk it over with someone you trust before you pay
