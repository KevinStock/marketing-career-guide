This worksheet helps you find which of four beginner-friendly marketing jobs suits you best. There are no right or wrong answers. Answer honestly, not with what sounds impressive.

> Prefer to work on a phone? The [Marketing Role Finder](@/role-finder/) has the same questions, saves your answers as you go, and scores them for you. A [fillable PDF](@/downloads/Marketing-Role-Finder-EN.pdf) is also available ([日本語](@/downloads/Marketing-Role-Finder-JA.pdf)).

## How to use this worksheet

**Time needed:** about 20 minutes for Part 1, plus four 20-minute tasks in Part 2. You can do Part 2 over several days.

**The four roles**

| Role | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| **SNS** — social media management (SNS運用) | Plan, create and post content for a company's Instagram, TikTok, X or LINE, and read how it performed |
| **Ads** — ad operations (広告運用) | Set up and adjust paid ads on Google, Yahoo!, LINE and Meta so each yen brings in more customers |
| **Content** — SEO and content (SEO / コンテンツマーケティング) | Write articles and pages that people find through search, and improve them over time |
| **Analytics** — data and CRM (アクセス解析 / CRM) | Track what visitors and customers do, find problems in the numbers, and report them clearly |

Do Part 1 and Part 2 first. Don't look at the scoring key in Part 3 until you finish them.

## Part 1: Questions

Choose the **one** answer closest to you for each question. If two feel equal, pick the one you'd choose on a tired day. Write your letters in the table at the end of this part.

1. On a free afternoon, which sounds most appealing?
    - A) Writing a long post about something you know well
    - B) Making a set of good-looking posts for a friend's shop

2. When you see an ad or post that really works, what do you notice first?

3. Something you made performed badly. What's your first reaction?

10. Which result would make you proudest?

## Part 2: Try the work

Answers about yourself only go so far; trying the real work tells you more. Spend about 20 minutes on each task below. Stop when the time is up, even if you're not finished. Quality doesn't matter here — what matters is how it felt.

All four tasks use the same imaginary business: **Café Hinata**, a small neighborhood café that also sells coffee beans online.

### Task 1 — SNS: Instagram posts

Café Hinata is launching a seasonal drink, a chestnut latte. Write three Instagram captions for it, each with hashtags. For each one, describe in one sentence what the photo or video should show.

### Task 2 — Ads: Budget and headlines

Here is last month's online shop data:

| Channel | Visitors | Purchases |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Instagram | 4,000 | 20 |
| Google search | 1,500 | 45 |
| LINE | 800 | 32 |
| Email newsletter | 300 | 3 |

1. Café Hinata has ¥30,000 extra for ads next month. How would you split it across these channels? Explain why in three sentences.
2. Write three Google search ad headlines for the coffee beans, each 15 Japanese characters or fewer.

### Task 3 — Content: Article outline

Plan a blog article for Café Hinata's website titled roughly “How to choose your first coffee grinder.” Write the title, five section headings, and one sentence on what each section says. Think about what a beginner would actually type into Google.

### Task 4 — Analytics: Find the problem

Here are eight weeks of online shop data:

| Week | Visitors | Purchases |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 2,100 | 42 |
| 2 | 2,200 | 44 |
| 3 | 2,050 | 41 |
| 4 | 2,150 | 43 |
| 5 | 2,200 | 18 |
| 6 | 2,100 | 20 |
| 7 | 2,250 | 45 |
| 8 | 2,150 | 43 |

1. What changed, and when?
2. List three possible causes.
3. What would you check first, and why?

## Part 4: What your result means

Your highest total shows where to start, not where you must stay. Many marketers move between these roles over their careers.

| If your top role is… | You probably enjoy… | Your first step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SNS** | Trends, visuals, writing short and catchy, talking with customers | Take the free LINEヤフー Marketing Skill Badge (LINE official account, Basic) and the Meta Blueprint beginner course. Start a practice Instagram account for an imaginary shop. |
| **Ads** | Fast results, testing, numbers tied to money, explaining results to others | Take the free Google Ads Search certification on Google Skillshop. Look for agency jobs marked 未経験OK. |
| **Content** | Writing, research, patient work that builds over months | Take the free Google Analytics certification, then write two sample articles on a free blog. |
| **Analytics** | Patterns, accuracy, spreadsheets, finding what others miss | Take the free Google Analytics certification. Consider ウェブ解析士 or 統計検定 Grade 3 next. |

**If two roles are close**, that's common, and some pairs are especially in demand:

- **SNS + Ads** → social media ads (SNS広告運用) — the most common beginner job.
- **Content + Analytics** → SEO marketer, who improves pages using search data.
- **Ads + Analytics** → performance marketer, focused on cost per customer.
- **SNS + Content** → content marketer for a brand's own channels.

**Check your Part 2 work**

Read these only after rating the tasks.

- **Task 2:** Instagram brings the most visitors but only about 0.5% buy. LINE (4%) and Google search (3%) turn visitors into buyers far better. A strong answer moves most of the money toward those two, or explains how to fix Instagram first.
- **Task 4:** In weeks 5 and 6, purchases fell by more than half while visitors stayed the same. So people still arrived but stopped buying. Good causes to check include a broken checkout or payment page, items out of stock, or a price change. Checking the checkout page first is a strong answer, because it is quick to test and costs money every day it is broken.

## Part 5: Reflect and talk it over

Write short answers to these questions. Then talk them through, along with your Part 2 work, with someone you trust.

1. Did your result surprise you? Why or why not?
2. Which task would you happily do again tomorrow, and which would you avoid?
3. Was anything harder than you expected? What would help you get better at it?
4. Is there anything in your life right now — time, money, location, family — that makes one role easier or harder to start?
5. What is one small step you can finish in the next two weeks?
