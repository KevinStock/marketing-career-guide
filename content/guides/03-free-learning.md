Start with free courses before you spend any money. The three platforms that run most targeted ads in Japan teach their own tools for free, in Japanese, with free online certificates. Finishing them in your first two months also shows you whether you enjoy the work.

## Platform courses

| Resource | What it covers | Cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Google Skillshop](https://sienca.jp/blog/marketing/google-ads-certification/) (Google広告認定資格) | Google Ads (nine certifications; start with 検索広告, Search) and Google Analytics 4 | Free | Online, any time. Agencies need certified staff to keep their Google Partner status, so this certificate helps you get hired. |
| [LINEヤフー マーケティングスキルバッジ](https://www.lycbiz.com/jp/support/lineyahoo-marketing-skill-badge/) | LINE official accounts and LINEヤフー広告 (LINE and Yahoo! JAPAN ads) | Free | Basic and Advanced levels; valid for one year. Uniquely Japanese, so it helps you stand out. |
| [Meta Blueprint](https://crexgroup.com/ja/marketing/career-learning/meta-blueprint-certification/) | Facebook and Instagram ads, targeting and measurement | Courses free; exams may charge a fee | Formal exams are booked through [Pearson VUE](https://www.pearsonvue.com/jp/ja/meta.html). Start with デジタルマーケティングアソシエイト. Not every exam is offered in Japanese. |

## Suggested order

1. Google Ads Search certification on Google Skillshop. Agencies value this one most.
2. Google Analytics 4 certification.
3. LINEヤフー Marketing Skill Badge, Basic level.
4. Meta Blueprint Digital Marketing Associate course.

If your top role is social media, do 3 and 4 first.

## Free public training: Hello Training

If you are between jobs, the government's ハロートレーニング (Hello Training) runs free 3–6 month web marketing courses designed to get people hired. It is the cheapest serious option, and many people don't know about it.

- **Who it's for:** 求職者支援訓練 (job-seeker support training) is free and certified by MHLW, for people who can't receive unemployment insurance ([Okayama course flyer](https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/okayama-roudoukyoku/content/contents/002246800.pdf)). If you are receiving unemployment benefits, ask Hello Work about 公共職業訓練 instead.
- **What's taught:** typical courses cover web marketing basics, SEO, social media operations, Canva graphics, landing pages, web writing and analytics. Some 6-month courses aim for Google Analytics, Google Ads Search and ウェブ解析士 certifications ([Nara course](https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/nara-roudoukyoku/content/contents/001896692.pdf)).
- **Online options:** e-learning courses exist for people raising children, caring for family, living far from a training center, or currently working. You need a computer with a camera and microphone ([Yamagata e-learning course](https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/yamagata-roudoukyoku/content/contents/002771886.pdf)). Several include building a portfolio site.
- **How to apply:** courses start on fixed dates, with application windows of five to six weeks. Search “ハロートレーニング Webマーケティング” plus your prefecture, then apply through your local Hello Work.

Public courses are broader than private schools, focus less on ad operations, and don't include real client work. Combine them with the platform courses above and the portfolio projects in Guide 6.
