Most beginners in Japan start in ad operations (広告運用) at an agency, then move to an in-house role after two or three years. Agencies hire beginners because they train people on many clients at once. In-house teams usually want proven results.

## The four entry roles

| Role (Japanese search term) | What you do day to day | A good fit if you… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Social media** · SNS運用 / SNS運用代行 | Plan posts, write captions, make simple graphics, read account analytics | Already post on Instagram, TikTok or X and follow trends |
| **Ad operations** · 広告運用 / 運用型広告 | Set up and adjust Google, Yahoo!, LINE, Meta and TikTok ads; report cost per customer (CPA) and return on ad spend (ROAS) | Like numbers, spreadsheets and quick results |
| **SEO and content** · SEOマーケター / コンテンツマーケティング | Research keywords, write articles, improve website pages | Enjoy writing and research |
| **Analytics and CRM** · アクセス解析 / CRM / MA | Build GA4 reports; segment LINE official account and email lists | Are careful, methodical and detail-oriented |

**Not sure which fits?** Take the [Marketing Role Finder](@/role-finder/). It has 12 questions and four short practice tasks, and it tells you which role to start with.

## Combinations in demand

If two roles score close for you, that's common. Some pairs are especially wanted:

- **Social media + ad operations** → social media ads (SNS広告運用). The most common beginner job.
- **SEO and content + analytics** → SEO marketer, who improves pages using search data.
- **Ad operations + analytics** → performance marketer, focused on cost per customer.
- **Social media + SEO and content** → content marketer for a brand's own channels.

## How careers usually grow

1. **Entry:** one of the four roles above, usually at an agency or as a freelancer.
2. **In-house (after two to three years):** you own one company's results across channels (インハウス / 事業会社). Employers want proof of results from agency or freelance work.
3. **Later:** team lead, or a specialist role such as performance marketer or SEO lead.

## Where you could be in a few years

A 2026 in-house listing for a large consumer brand asked for agency or in-house ad operations experience across Google (Search, P-MAX, YouTube), Yahoo!, social and TVer. It also asked for GA4 analysis and spreadsheet skills such as pivot tables and VLOOKUP ([listing](https://directscout.recruit.co.jp/job_descriptions/8532346)). Use it as a map of the skills to build toward.

## Pay

The government's job-information site (job tag) puts advertised wages for web marketing at about ¥264,000 a month nationally (FY2024). Company listings range widely, from roughly ¥3M to ¥10M a year ([summary](https://remedy-tokyo.co.jp/media/10948/)). Beginner agency roles commonly advertise ¥3.5M–5M.

## Your background counts

Backgrounds that transfer well include sales (especially B2B), customer support, e-commerce operations, writing and web production ([Remedy](https://remedy-tokyo.co.jp/media/10948/)). Describe your past work in marketing terms:

- **Sales:** you know how to move a customer from interest to purchase.
- **Customer support:** you know what customers ask and what frustrates them.
- **E-commerce or retail:** you have handled products, prices and promotions.
- **Writing or web production:** you can already make pages and posts.

## Timing

Japanese recruiters note that career-changers are hired on potential when young. If you are 30 or older, start applying early rather than waiting to finish one more course ([Mynavi Agent](https://mynavi-agent.jp/knowledge/it/1278.html)).
