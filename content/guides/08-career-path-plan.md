The standard plan takes you from no experience to applying for entry-level jobs in about six months, studying part time. If you can study full time, the fast track takes about three months.

<!-- roadmap -->

The phases overlap on purpose. Start your portfolio while your course is still running, and keep side work going while you apply.

## Choose your pace

| Track | Study time | Total | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | About 10–15 hours a week | About 6 months | Studying while you work |
| Fast track | About 30 hours a week or more | About 3 months | Between jobs, or able to study full time |

The fast track usually means self-study or a school you can take at your own pace. Hello Training courses run on fixed 3–6 month schedules (Guide 3).

## Phase checklists

Tick each item as you finish it. Your ticks are saved in this browser. Finish every item in a phase before you move on to the next.

### Phase 1: Choose your route

Standard: weeks 1–2 · Fast: week 1

- [ ] Take the [Marketing Role Finder](@/role-finder/) and note your top role
- [ ] Ask Hello Work about your employment insurance status
- [ ] Choose free training, a school or self-study (Guide 5)
- [ ] If you are using a refund program, register before the course start date

### Phase 2: Free platform foundations

Standard: months 1–2 · Fast: weeks 1–4

- [ ] Google Ads Search certification
- [ ] Google Analytics 4 certification
- [ ] LINEヤフー Marketing Skill Badge, Basic
- [ ] Meta Blueprint Digital Marketing Associate course
- [ ] Read one trade article a day (Guide 1)

### Phase 3: Structured course and ウェブ解析士

Standard: months 2–4 · Fast: weeks 3–8

- [ ] Start Hello Training, a school course, or a self-study plan
- [ ] Pass ウェブ解析士 (40–60 hours of study; Guide 4)

### Phase 4: Build your portfolio

Standard: months 3–5 · Fast: weeks 6–10

- [ ] Practice piece 1
- [ ] Practice piece 2
- [ ] One real campaign on a ¥10,000–30,000 budget
- [ ] Write up all three pieces (Guide 6)
- [ ] Optional: your first paid side job

### Phase 5: Job search

Standard: months 5–6 · Fast: weeks 8–12

- [ ] Register with one or two recruiting agents
- [ ] Update your 職務経歴書 (Guide 7)
- [ ] Apply to 未経験OK roles every week
- [ ] Practice the six interview questions in Guide 7 out loud
- [ ] Keep side work going while you apply

## How to go faster

- Overlap the phases. Start the portfolio while your course is still running.
- Start applying as soon as you have one real campaign, not three.
- If you are 30 or older, start the job search by month 4.
- Choose a school that includes real client work and job support, so phases 3–5 happen together.

## Signs you're stuck

If any of these is true, pause and talk it through with someone who knows your goals:

- You are collecting certificates but haven't built anything real by month 4.
- You can't explain your results with numbers.
- A side-job program promising guaranteed income asks you to pay upfront.
- You keep putting off applying to finish “one more course.”

## Regular check-ins

Every two weeks, set aside 30 minutes to review this checklist and one portfolio piece or result. It is easier to stay on track when you report to someone, so share your progress with a person who will check in on you.
