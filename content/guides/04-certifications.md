Get the free platform certificates first (Guide 3). Then get one paid Japanese credential: ウェブ解析士 (Web Analyst). Beyond that, extra certificates add little, because employers care more about real results.

## Japanese certifications

ウェブ解析士 is the best-known Japanese marketing certification, and real job listings name it as a plus ([Mynavi listing](https://tenshoku.mynavi.jp/list/o1G105/)). Japanese recruiters also say no web role requires a certificate, and that experience and results matter more ([Mynavi Agent](https://mynavi-agent.jp/knowledge/it/1278.html)).

| Certification | Fee (tax incl.) | Effort | Worth it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ウェブ解析士](https://atsoho.com/certifications/web-analyst) (WACA) | ¥22,000 | 40–60 hours; about 60% pass; online, 60 questions plus a GA report | Yes, this is the one to get |
| [上級ウェブ解析士](https://atsoho.com/certifications/web-analyst-master) | ¥88,000 | 100–200 hours; about a 2-month course | Later, once you have a job. Your employer may pay. |
| [ネットマーケティング検定](https://cad-kenkyujo.com/digital-marketing/) | ¥6,200 | Light; online | Optional; a cheap check of your vocabulary |
| [マーケティング・ビジネス実務検定](https://cad-kenkyujo.com/digital-marketing/) | ¥6,270 (C) – ¥12,760 (A) | Light to moderate | Optional; general marketing theory |
| [統計検定](https://cad-kenkyujo.com/digital-marketing/) 3級 / 2級 | ¥6,000 / ¥7,000 | Moderate | Useful if you lean toward analytics |

The same association (WACA) also certifies SNSマネージャー and ウェブ広告マネージャー. As of March 2026 it reported 14,346 Web Analyst holders ([Hokkaido Shimbun](https://www.hokkaido-np.co.jp/article/1305828/)). Check the current fee on WACA's own website before you pay, because other sites can be out of date.

## Which certificates fit your role

| Your top role | Get these first (free) | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social media | LINEヤフー badge (Basic), Meta Blueprint | ウェブ解析士 |
| Ad operations | Google Ads Search, Meta Blueprint | ウェブ解析士 |
| SEO and content | Google Analytics 4 | ウェブ解析士 |
| Analytics and CRM | Google Analytics 4 | ウェブ解析士, then 統計検定 3級 |

## Tips

- List each certificate on your 職務経歴書 with the month you passed it.
- The LINEヤフー badge is valid for one year, and other platform certificates can expire too. Check the dates before you apply for jobs.
- Stop collecting certificates once you have the free platform ones and ウェブ解析士. Put that time into the portfolio (Guide 6).
