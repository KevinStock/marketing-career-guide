Search for roles marked 未経験歓迎 or 未経験OK, and register with at least one recruiting agent. In Japan, agents are free for job seekers and often help you write your 職務経歴書 (career history document).

## Where to look

| doda, マイナビ転職, エン転職 | Many listings, including 未経験OK ad-operations roles ([example on doda](https://careerconnection.jp/job/A0223015205769-detail), [エン転職 marketing](https://employment.en-japan.com/k_113300/s_marketing/)) |

## What hiring managers look for

Japanese recruiters say to explain every project in the same five parts: 施策, 仮説, 改善, 結果 and 再現性 (what you did, your hypothesis, what you changed, the result, and why it would work again) ([Remedy](https://remedy-tokyo.co.jp/media/10948/)). Guide 6 shows how to write each portfolio piece this way. Bring your portfolio of 5–10 pages to every interview ([Recruit Agent](https://www.r-agent.com/guide/start/12572/)).

## Your 職務経歴書

- Describe your past jobs in marketing terms (Guide 2 has examples). Sales, customer support, e-commerce, writing and web production all transfer well.
- List your certificates with the month you passed each one.
- Add your real campaign and side-work results, with numbers.
- Ask your agent to review it before you apply.

## Practice for interviews

Interviewers often ask you to explain a campaign. Practice answering these out loud about your own portfolio pieces. Good answers are short, use a number, and say why.

1. Who exactly is this ad for? Describe that person.
2. What was the goal of this campaign, and did it reach it?
3. What did one customer cost (CPA), and is that good for this business?
4. If the budget were cut in half tomorrow, what would you stop first, and why?
5. What would you test next, and what result would change your mind?
6. Is this sponsored post labeled correctly under the stealth-marketing rule (Guide 1)?

## Timing

Start applying once you have one real campaign in your portfolio. You don't need all three pieces first. If you are 30 or older, start earlier rather than waiting to finish one more course ([Mynavi Agent](https://mynavi-agent.jp/knowledge/it/1278.html)).
